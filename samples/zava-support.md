# Zava Retail — original fictional evidence

All records and terms below are synthetic demonstration content. They are not
customer data, legal advice, real recall instructions or real product guidance.
Stable section IDs are source document IDs used by the Studio corpus.

## POL-RET-2026-01 — Ordinary returns

Ordinary returns require proof of purchase within 30 days. At 45 days that
ordinary window has ended. This rule does not replace the defective-goods
process.

## POL-DEF-2026-02 — Defective goods process

For a battery which stopped charging after 45 days, contact support with the
receipt, serial number and lot. Assessment under the defective-goods exception
is a separate process, not an automatic refund.

## WAR-BAT-2026-01 — Battery warranty

Manufacturing-defect coverage lasts 12 months from purchase. Eligibility depends
on purchase date, model and receipt. Exclusions include water damage, misuse and
normal wear. Neither a charging failure nor age alone proves eligibility.

## MAN-CHARGE-1001 — Charging manual

For a battery which stopped charging, inspect the charger and BAT-18V-A lot.
Stop use if overheating and contact support. Never bypass a damaged battery
safety circuit.

## BOM-2026-01 — Battery bill of materials

DRILL-1001 uses BAT-18V-A containing CELL-B17 from SUP-017. Selected lots are
LOT-2407-A and LOT-2407-B. DRILL-1002 uses BAT-18V-B containing CELL-C20 and
LOT-2501-C; it is not automatically affected by the B-17 incident.

## INC-2026-017 — B-17 incident

The synthetic supplier battery-cell defect concerns CELL-B17 in LOT-2407-A and
LOT-2407-B. Affected product lineage is DRILL-1001 → BAT-18V-A → CELL-B17.
Incident evidence does not establish that every battery or supplier part is
defective.

## SUP-BUL-017 — Supplier bulletin

SUP-017 confirms charging failures in CELL-B17 in LOT-2407-A and LOT-2407-B.
CELL-X10 is a different part from the same supplier and is unaffected.
