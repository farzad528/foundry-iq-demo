# Zava Business Travel

Document: HR-004, revision 2. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: People Operations and Finance. Related documents: FIN-002 and HR-003.

This is original fictional demonstration content, not a real employer policy or legal advice.

## Before booking

Business travel needs a documented business purpose, cost center, dates, estimated total, and manager approval. Use the approved booking channel. International trips also need a destination risk review. Travel for an employee's personal remote-work preference is not business travel.

Flights are economy for journeys shorter than six scheduled hours. Premium economy for a journey of six hours or longer requires director approval before booking. Business class is not a default entitlement.

## Expenses

FIN-002 controls reimbursement amounts, documentation, and currency conversion. This travel policy does not add an allowance on top of Finance's daily caps.

UK hotel reimbursement is capped at GBP 180 per night outside London and GBP 230 in London, excluding separately itemized taxes. A documented exception must be approved before booking. The hotel cap is not the meal allowance.

Personal extensions are allowed only when they do not increase Zava's cost or create an unapproved work location. The employee pays personal nights, activities, companions, and incremental fares.

## Combining leave and travel

Record annual-leave dates separately. Working during a personal extension abroad requires a separate HR-003 approval and counts toward international remote-work limits. A business-trip approval does not authorize it.

## Missing evidence

An itinerary alone is not approval. If the manager approval, director exception, Finance documentation, or international-work authorization is missing, name the missing item and the policy owner. Do not tell the employee to book first and regularize it later.
