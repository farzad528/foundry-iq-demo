# Zava International Remote Work

Document: HR-003, revision 4. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: Mobility. Supersedes revision 3 dated 1 July 2026.

This is original fictional demonstration content, not a real employer policy or legal advice.

## Who can apply

Permanent employees with at least twelve complete months of continuous service may apply. The arrangement is discretionary, not an entitlement. Contractors and employees below the service threshold are ineligible under this policy; a line manager cannot waive that threshold.

The employee must have satisfactory performance, a managed device, multifactor authentication, and a role that can be performed without moving restricted customer data. The policy does not provide immigration or tax advice.

## Places and duration

The demonstration approved-destination list is Portugal, Spain, Ireland, and the Netherlands. A destination absent from that list is not approved by inference.

The maximum is 20 working days in a rolling twelve-month period, with no more than ten consecutive working days per trip. Weekends and approved annual leave are not remote-working days. The total limit and consecutive-trip limit both apply.

For example, one 20-working-day uninterrupted work trip is not allowed, even if the employee has used no other international remote-work days.

## Request and approvals

Submit the exact destination, dates, workdays, duties, and data-access needs at least 15 working days before departure. The manager first approves team coverage. Mobility then reviews the location and proposed arrangement. Information Security approves access and device safeguards. All three written approvals must be recorded before travel.

Do not book non-refundable travel before final approval. Remote work for personal convenience is not business travel and the company does not pay its travel or accommodation costs.

## Existing arrangements and return from leave

An approved domestic hybrid pattern is not international-work approval. Returning from parental leave or using the four-week HR-002 transition does not waive the service requirement or the ten-consecutive-working-day cap.

Revision 3 allowed requests ten working days before departure. That notice period is superseded: use the revision 4 requirement of 15 working days for new requests.

## Unresolved circumstances

Mobility must review an unavailable local addendum, visa or tax concern, restricted-data access, or a destination outside the list. An open review is not approval. If an employee is ineligible, explain why and distinguish a domestic flexibility request from international work.
