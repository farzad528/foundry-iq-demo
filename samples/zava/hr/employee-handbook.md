# Zava Employee Handbook

Document: HR-001, revision 3. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: People Operations. Applies to: fictional Zava employees.

This is original fictional demonstration content, not a real employer policy or legal advice.

## Eligibility and precedence

A permanent employee works under a Zava employment contract. Contractors, agency workers, and supplier staff are not employees for benefits eligibility. Local statutory entitlements take precedence over this handbook. A written, approved local addendum takes precedence over a global policy; a manager's informal assurance does not.

Benefits eligibility begins on the employment start date unless a particular benefit specifies a service requirement. Probation is six months. Completing probation does not replace the twelve-month service requirement for international remote work in HR-003.

## Working arrangements

The standard full-time schedule is five working days and 40 hours per week. Hybrid teams normally attend their designated office two days each week; the team records its agreed days. A domestic flexible-work arrangement needs written manager approval and a review date.

Domestic hybrid work, international remote work, business travel, and annual leave are different arrangements. Approval for one does not authorize the others. HR-003 governs working from another country; HR-004 governs business travel.

## Joining and equipment

People Operations completes identity and employment checks. IT provisions a managed laptop, multifactor authentication, and least-privilege access. A manager approves role-specific systems. Employees must not use a personal device for customer data.

The standard home-office allowance is GBP 350 once every 24 months for UK employees. Finance reimburses approved equipment against itemized receipts. The allowance is not cash compensation and unused amounts are not carried forward.

## Decisions and exceptions

People Operations decides benefit eligibility. The Mobility team assesses international work requests. Finance decides reimbursement under its current expense policy. A manager cannot waive a security control, statutory restriction, or the international-work service requirement.

Record the applicable policy revision, decision owner, and approval before committing spend or changing location. Where a necessary local addendum or approval is absent, report the missing evidence rather than assume approval.
