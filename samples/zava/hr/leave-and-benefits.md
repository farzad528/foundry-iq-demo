# Zava Leave and Benefits

Document: HR-002, revision 2. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: People Operations. Related documents: HR-001 and HR-003.

This is original fictional demonstration content, not a real employer policy or legal advice.

## UK annual leave

A full-time UK employee receives 25 annual-leave days per calendar year, plus applicable public holidays. Part-time entitlement is prorated by contracted working days. A new joiner's annual allowance is prorated from the start date and rounded up to the nearest half day.

Employees request leave in the HR system. The manager checks coverage and approves it before bookings. Up to five unused days may carry into the following year with manager approval and must normally be used by 31 March. Statutory carry-over exceptions require People Operations review.

Annual leave is time away from work. It cannot be counted as international remote-working days.

## Parental leave and returning to work

Eligible permanent employees may receive Zava's enhanced parental benefit after six complete months of continuous service. Local statutory rights still apply regardless of eligibility for the enhancement. People Operations must confirm the applicable local addendum and individual entitlement; this document does not establish a specific employee's remaining balance.

A returning employee can request a four-week transition at 80% of contracted hours with 100% of normal base pay. The employee and manager agree coverage, dates, and objectives; People Operations records approval before the transition starts. This is not an automatic change to the permanent employment contract.

The return-to-work transition does not waive HR-003's twelve-month service requirement for international remote work.

## Learning and wellbeing

The annual learning allowance is GBP 1,200 per UK permanent employee. The manager approves a job-relevant course before purchase. Mandatory compliance training is funded separately and does not reduce this allowance.

The wellbeing allowance is GBP 300 per calendar year. Itemized receipts and the current Finance expense categories are required. Learning and wellbeing allowances cannot be combined to buy an otherwise ineligible item.

## Evidence needed for an individual answer

Confirm employee classification, work country, start date, contracted schedule, leave already taken, and approvals. Do not invent a leave balance or promise a statutory entitlement without the relevant local record.
