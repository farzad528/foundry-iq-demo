# Zava Expense Reimbursement

Document: FIN-002, revision 3. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: Finance Operations. Related documents: HR-002 and HR-004.

This is original fictional demonstration content. Amounts are demonstration rules, not financial advice.

## Submission and evidence

Submit expenses within 30 calendar days of the transaction. Include an itemized receipt, date, business purpose, cost center, original currency, and approval reference. A card statement by itself is not an itemized receipt.

For a missing receipt under GBP 25, a signed missing-receipt declaration and manager approval may be accepted. A missing receipt of GBP 25 or more requires Finance Operations review; reimbursement is not automatic.

Finance uses the card provider's settled rate for a corporate-card purchase. For an approved personal-card purchase, use the Finance-published rate for the transaction date. Do not select whichever rate gives the largest reimbursement.

## UK business-travel limits

| Category | Limit | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meals and non-alcoholic drinks | GBP 45 per travel day | Actual eligible cost, not a flat per diem |
| Hotel outside London | GBP 180 per night | Separately itemized taxes excluded from cap |
| Hotel in London | GBP 230 per night | Separately itemized taxes excluded from cap |
| Personal car mileage | GBP 0.35 per business mile | Route and business purpose required |

Remove breakfast or another meal already included in the hotel rate or supplied by an event from the claim; do not claim it twice. Alcohol, fines, companions' costs, and personal sightseeing are not reimbursable.

## Exceptions and training

An above-cap hotel needs a documented pre-booking exception from the department director and Finance Controller. An after-the-fact manager note is not equivalent.

The HR-002 learning and wellbeing limits still apply to those categories. A valid receipt does not make an over-limit or unapproved expense eligible.

## Example

A London hotel at GBP 245 before tax exceeds the GBP 230 cap by GBP 15 per night. Without the required pre-booking exception, do not state that the full room charge is approved. Separately evaluate meals against the GBP 45 actual-cost cap and remove any included breakfast.
