# Zava Purchasing and Approval Matrix

Document: FIN-001, revision 5. Effective: 1 October 2026.
Owner: Finance Controller. Related documents: FIN-003 and FIN-004.

This is original fictional demonstration content. All amounts and business records are invented.

## Calculate the commitment

Approval is based on the total first-year committed cost in USD: subscription or license charges, required implementation, onboarding, and other unavoidable fees. Exclude recoverable tax. Do not split purchases, purchase orders, or invoices to avoid a threshold.

For a non-USD commitment, Finance records the approved planning rate and conversion date before the purchase order is approved.

## Approval matrix

| Total first-year commitment | Required financial approvals | Competitive evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to and including USD 5,000 | Cost-center manager | One documented quote |
| Above USD 5,000 through USD 25,000 | Department director and Finance Controller | Two comparable quotes |
| Above USD 25,000 through USD 100,000 | Department director, Finance Controller, and CFO | Three comparable quotes |
| Above USD 100,000 | CFO and Executive Purchasing Committee | Formal sourcing review |

An approved sole-source exception can replace the quote requirement, but not the financial approvals or available-budget check.

## Budget and vendor gates

FIN-003 must show sufficient uncommitted budget for the entire first-year commitment. A budget shortfall blocks purchase-order issuance until a signed reallocation or budget increase is recorded.

Every new vendor needs Finance vendor onboarding. A vendor processing Zava or customer personal data additionally needs a signed data-processing agreement and Information Security review before a purchase order or contract signature.

Security and Legal reviews do not replace financial approval. A quote, invoice, or purchase-order draft is not authority to incur spend.

## Worked demonstration

A USD 18,500 annual analytics subscription plus mandatory USD 6,000 onboarding is a USD 24,500 commitment. It needs department-director and Finance-Controller approval and two comparable quotes. If the vendor processes personal data, the data-processing agreement and Information Security gate also apply.

The FIN-003 ENG-420 budget has USD 22,000 uncommitted: this example is USD 2,500 over budget. It cannot proceed merely because USD 24,500 is below the CFO's purchase-approval threshold.
